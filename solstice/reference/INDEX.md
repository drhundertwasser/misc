# Reference faces for the solstice film

Public-domain and Creative Commons documentary portraits, chosen as the register we want: real, unposed, haggard, weathered, amateur. Not Hollywood. Sources and licences below; thumbnails in `final/`, board in `board_final.jpg`.

## What they teach for the prompts

- Skin is uneven: sun-lines, broken capillaries, chapped lips, dirt in the creases, no symmetry, no retouching.
- Hair is unwashed and unstyled; hats and wraps are worn-in, not costumes.
- Eyes do the work: wide, still, slightly wet; mouths closed or barely open; no performed emotion.
- Bodies are thin or heavy in real ways; clothes hang; children look older than their age.
- Light is flat daylight or low sun, not beauty light. Depth of field is modest, backgrounds plain.
- The camera is at eye level and close, often slightly below for children, and the subject is not centred perfectly.
